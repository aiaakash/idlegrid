idlegrid — from your desk to production

Three stages, one codebase. The architecture was deliberately shaped so that going from one Mac → two Macs → thousands changes where processes live, not how they're written. The key property making this work: providers always dial out to the coordinator — the coordinator is the only component that ever needs a reachable address.

The whole point of the v0 design: because providers dial out and the coordinator is stateless-ish, "production" is mostly (1) point the daemon at a domain, (2) add Postgres + TLS + billing around the same Go binary. No architectural rewrite — the hard 20% (protocol, scheduler, failure semantics) is already proven on your desk.
